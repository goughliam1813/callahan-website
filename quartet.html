Native apps, not just websites

Quartet QE drives native Android apps as well as websites and phone browsers, from the same plain-English story. One story can also check the APIs, queues and databases behind the screen.

A story in, proof out

01Story

Type it, point at a file, or pull a ticket from Jira, Azure DevOps, GitHub or GitLab.

02Tests

The AI reads your contracts and telemetry, reuses your steps, writes the tests and validates them.

03Replay

The recording is the test. Reruns, in CI or on your laptop, use no AI.

04Approve

An independent review and a traceability matrix go to one page. A person approves.

TargetWhat it does

Reports you can hand over

Every run writes a branded, self-contained report. Open it in a browser, attach it to a ticket, or publish it from Jenkins.

PartWhat it shows
ResultsEach test against the story it came from.
ScreenshotsA picture of every step, for each target.
ReviewAn independent check that reads the tests before they run.
